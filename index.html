Our history — Specialists in digital fraud investigation and crypto asset tracing

Our history and mission at Recovera

At Recovera, we are a team of young experts in economics, digital forensics, and cryptocurrency traceability, motivated by a personal experience with crypto scams. Seeing how people close to us lost money and received no clear or effective help, we decided to form an organization specialized in tracing stolen digital assets and supporting victims of online fraud.

Unlike those who claim "miraculous" recoveries, at Recovera we use serious investigation methodologies and forensic technical analysis to reconstruct the route of the funds, providing transparent and useful information to understand what really happened.

How Recovera was born

After hearing phrases like "you've lost it, it can't be found" at police stations, we realized that it is possible to find valuable clues in digital transactions through blockchain analysis techniques and coordination with legal professionals. Thus Recovera was born, with the mission of helping individuals and companies affected by cryptocurrency scams, online financial fraud, forex, and other digital crimes.

The importance of acting fast

Digital scams are growing in sophistication and volume. In Spain and Europe, every year thousands of cases of fraud with cryptocurrencies and other digital assets leave victims without resources or clear information. Recovering and understanding what happened can make the difference to move forward legally or manage resources wisely.

At Recovera we help identify suspicious transactions, analyze forensic patterns, and offer clear technical reports that can be useful for legal actions or complaints before competent authorities.
